# OpenGadda. Open Data

The project behind OpenGadda, Open Data, was built to solve a very common problem in digital methodologies: what can we access and produce when our sources are under copyright?

Focusing on the case of Carlo Emilio Gadda, whose works and documents are still under copyright, the project aims to create a platform where users can browse the author's archives, bibliographies, libraries and letters.

This is possible due to the wide variety of meta-textual documents related to the author and his own production (e.g., documents regarding the set-up of the author's archives) that are not under copyright, unlike his works and personal documents.

From said documents, we extracted metadata related to the author's works and production to create three outcomes:

1. an interconnected digital platform for users to construct their own didactic journey, browsing and connecting data through authors, dates and works.

2. a timeline, based on the visualisation concept of storyline, not just to show the temporal progression of the author's life and production but also the different agents involved.

3. a social network, based on the author's epistolary correspondence to showcase his relationships over the years.

All of this aims to show new insights into the author's life, relationships and production.
